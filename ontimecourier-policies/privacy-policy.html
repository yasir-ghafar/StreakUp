1. Introduction

On Time Courier ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your personal information when you use our website at www.ontimecourier.co.uk, our courier and delivery services, and when you contact us.

We process personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Who We Are

Data Controller: On Time Courier

Contact: support@ontimecourier.co.uk | +44 20 8004 8011

On Time Courier provides domestic courier and parcel delivery services across the United Kingdom, including same-day delivery, standard shipping, and corporate logistics solutions.

3. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identity and contact data: name, email address, phone number, and delivery/collection addresses.
  • Shipment data: tracking numbers, package details, delivery instructions, and proof of delivery information.
  • Account data: login credentials and preferences if you create an account with us.
  • Communication data: messages sent via our contact form, email correspondence, and customer support interactions.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, and website usage data collected through cookies (see our Cookie Policy).
  • Payment data: billing information processed securely through our payment providers. We do not store full card details on our servers.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To process and fulfil courier bookings, pickups, and deliveries.
  • To provide real-time package tracking and delivery notifications.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide 24/7 customer support.
  • To process payments and issue invoices or receipts.
  • To manage insurance claims for lost or damaged packages.
  • To improve our website, services, and customer experience.
  • To send service-related communications (e.g. delivery updates).
  • To comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: to perform our courier services agreement with you.
  • Legitimate interests: to operate and improve our business, prevent fraud, and ensure service quality.
  • Consent: for marketing communications and non-essential cookies, where applicable.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

6. Sharing Your Information

We may share your data with:

  • Delivery drivers and logistics partners involved in fulfilling your shipment.
  • Payment processors to handle transactions securely.
  • IT and hosting providers who support our website and systems.
  • Insurance providers in connection with claims for lost or damaged goods.
  • Regulatory or law enforcement authorities when required by law.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy. Shipment records are typically retained for up to 7 years for accounting and legal purposes. Marketing data is retained until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe.

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal exceptions).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent at any time for consent-based processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@ontimecourier.co.uk.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes encryption, secure servers, and access controls.

10. International Transfers

Your data is primarily processed within the United Kingdom. If we transfer data outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with UK data protection law.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
